Mathematical Methodology & Approach Explanation

Algorithm: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression with Statistical Prediction Intervals.

Mathematical Formulation: Fits linear regression \(\hat{y} = mx + c\) where \(x\) represents consecutive billing intervals and \(y\) denotes aggregated gross billing revenue. Slope \(m\) and intercept \(c\) are determined by minimizing sum of squared residuals \(\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2\).

Confidence Interval Synthesis: Future variance bounds are computed as \(\hat{y} \pm 1.96 \cdot S_e \cdot \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{n} + \frac{(x - \bar{x})^2}{\sum(x_i - \bar{x})^2}}\) to model future uncertainty.
